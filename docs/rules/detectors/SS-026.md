# SS-026 — Inconsistent or inverted TTL threshold and extend_to arguments

- **Severity:** medium
- **Confidence:** high
- **Category:** storage

## Problem

A call to `extend_ttl` passes a `threshold` larger than `extend_to` (or
`extend_to == 0`). The Soroban host requires `threshold <= extend_to` and
panics at runtime when the arguments are inverted.

## How it is detected

The detector resolves integer literals and file-level `const` definitions for
the last two arguments `(threshold, extend_to)` of `extend_ttl`, `set_ttl`, and
`bump` calls, reporting any call where `threshold > extend_to` or
`extend_to == 0`.

## Limitations

- Arithmetic constant expressions (for example `DAY_LEDGERS * 7`) and
  cross-module constants are not evaluated.

## False positives

- None when `threshold` and `extend_to` are literal integers or direct constants.

## Remediation

Ensure `threshold <= extend_to` and `extend_to > 0` in every `extend_ttl` call.
